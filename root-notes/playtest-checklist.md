# iPhone playtest checklist (after every Build paste) — v5-21 candidate (v5-20 copy frozen at v520/playtest-checklist-v5-20-frozen.md)
Run on Maxwell's iPhone from the Home Screen icon. Tick each; note anything that fails with a screenshot. About 10 minutes.

## Pictures
1. Play one run in each zone: every picture card can be named at a glance; no two choices fit the picture (crib vs bottle, desk vs chair, the 4 digital clocks 5:30 / 9:30 / 11:00 / 7:30).
2. Color cards: purple, pink and brown each sit with clearly different colors (no near-hue choices). The circle card shows a blue circle you can see on the cream frame.

## Titles vs buttons
3. Every screen title is a flat gold ribbon: no bevel, shadow or press. Tapping it does nothing. Only things that do something look like buttons. The Settings gear matches #28.

## Map
4. Map > zone > back to Map, five times, across Phonics, Baby and Hospital and Maxwell Says: every tile icon and emblem is there every time.
5. Trophy counter and the orange streak flame are big and easy to read; the three enlarged trophies look clearly different.

## My Stuff
6. Every four-across row (tabs and grid) fills the screen edge to edge with even small gaps; no awkward side margins, in portrait.
7. Not-yet jewels and gear are full color with a small lock icon top-right and "Yours at level N" (Thai ได้รับเมื่อถึงเลเวล N). No "locked" or "unlock" words anywhere.
8. Each gear piece shows its name under it (e.g. Sunrise Shield, Little Stone Sling); names read at arm's length; no two names repeat.
9. Tap an earned item: it applies instantly on the avatar, with a short glow and soft chime; no lag, flicker or layout jump. Double-tap applies it once.

## In-game bar, cheer and nod
10. Three-bar (iPhone SE): during a run the top row is three equal blocks with even gaps: LEFT a big jewel count with the coins in two rows of 5 under it; CENTER her large portrait holding her weapon, class line under it; RIGHT level and XP bar, the round stone Settings button alone at bottom-right. The picture, Thai line, four choices and the hint/Got it row all fit below without scrolling; the picture still reads at a glance. Tapping the portrait or jewels does nothing. The Map top bar uses the same three blocks.
11. Right answer: the portrait briefly raises its arms. Wrong answer: a small encouraging nod (head tilts up, about 600 ms), never a droop. Cute, never mocking.
12. With iOS Reduce Motion on, the portrait does not move.
13. Equip a new item in My Stuff, start a run: the bar shows it right away.

## Leaf (Words of Jesus)
14. Fresh install: tapping the leaf (after the first hint) alternates encouragement and Jesus' words. She has exactly 2: John 15:9 and Luke 12:7.
15. Every verse shown is a whole verse, nothing cut off; English bold, Thai under it, small gloss line. John 10:11 shows both sentences. John 14:1 and John 8:12 show their quote marks exactly as printed.
16. Leaf words are shown, never spoken, on the question screen; the reveal's leaf gives encouragement only. On verse screens, tap-to-hear speaks the English only, never the Thai or the (ESV)/(THSV11) tags.
17. After both are seen: one gentle "You've read all of Jesus' words for now." bubble; no timer or countdown; the next tap starts them over.
18. Reach level 4 (Little Stone Sling): a third verse (John 14:1) joins. The total never goes past 6.

## Grace and privacy
19. Verses are never purchasable: no price, cost, shop, buy button, or coin spend near any verse, scroll or leaf word; nothing is gated on spending.
20. No timers, energy, lives, leaderboards, offers or push prompts anywhere.
21. No phone numbers, addresses, emails, surnames, due date or inn name on any screen or in Backup; no 911, "Call Max" or "Show the Nurse".
