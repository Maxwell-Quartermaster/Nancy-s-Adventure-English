**Quartermaster:** How the layers stack
- Layer 1, the archetype, is identity: who the bot is. It sets the color, silhouette, prop and personality, and it's what shows up on the shirt.
- Layer 2, the RPG role, is the flavor: how the bot moves. It sets the back pose and the energy. The tank braces forward, the healer reaches out, control points and directs, support hands off or builds, and DPS lunges or aims. The role shows only through the pose. No role words or icons go on the shirt, which keeps the text rule (name plus long role description).
- Party formation, for a group image or the dashboard later: Tank in front (Watchman), DPS on the flank (Auditor), Control in the middle (Chancellor), and Support and Healer behind (Quartermaster, Instructor). A balanced party of five.
- Chancellor's archetype action picks pair with these roles: Watchman raising the lantern on the wall (tank, lookout), Instructor handing out glowing pages (healer), Chancellor flipping a coin and pointing to give the call (control), Quartermaster hammering a block into place (support), Auditor's magnifier glint and scales (precision DPS).
