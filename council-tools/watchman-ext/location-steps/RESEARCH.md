**Watchman:** ## 1. Prenatal clinic visit
**Watchman:** Nancy has a routine visit, about 7 months along. Sam can come with her.
**Watchman:** ### Step 1: Check in at the front desk
**Watchman:** - SAY: "I have an appointment."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "My name is Nancy."
**Watchman:** - HEAR (receptionist): "What is your birthday?"
**Watchman:** - SAY: "I need a Thai interpreter."
**Watchman:** - HEAR (receptionist): "Please have a seat."
**Watchman:** ### Step 2: Weight and blood pressure
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Please step on the scale."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Is my weight okay?"
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Roll up your sleeve, please."
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Your blood pressure is good."
**Watchman:** ### Step 3: Urine sample
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "We need a urine sample."
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Please use this cup."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Where is the bathroom?"
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Here is my cup."
**Watchman:** ### Step 4: Belly check, heartbeat, ultrasound (only on some visits)
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Please lie down here."
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "This gel is a little cold."
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Let's listen to the heartbeat."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Is my baby okay?"
**Watchman:** - HEAR (nurse): "Your baby looks healthy."
**Watchman:** ### Step 5: Talk with the doctor
**Watchman:** - HEAR (doctor): "How are you feeling today?"
**Watchman:** - HEAR (doctor): "Is the baby moving a lot?"
**Watchman:** - SAY: "The baby kicks a lot."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "I have a question."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Please speak more slowly."
**Watchman:** ### Step 6: Make the next appointment
**Watchman:** - HEAR (receptionist): "Come back in two weeks."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Can I come Tuesday morning?"
**Watchman:** - HEAR (receptionist): "Here is your appointment card."
**Watchman:** - SAY: "Thank you. See you soon."
**Watchman:** ### Sources
**Watchman:** - https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/your-pregnancy-care/your-antenatal-appointments/
**Watchman:**   - Supports: 28 week appointment: "ask to check your blood pressure", "ask to test your pee", "ask to measure your bump", "discuss your baby's movements". Also: "If you have a partner, they can come to appointments if you would like them to."
**Watchman:** - https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/pregnancy-week-by-week/in-depth/prenatal-care/art-20044882
**Watchman:**   - Supports: "your health care provider will measure your weight and height"; "you might be able to hear your baby's heartbeat with a small device, called a Doppler"; "Your prenatal appointments are an ideal time to discuss questions you have."
**Watchman:** - https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/having-a-baby
**Watchman:**   - Supports: "As your pregnancy continues, you may visit your ob-gyn more often. At your visits, you can ask any questions you may have." and "You also may have a urine test".
**Watchman:** ### What varies
**Watchman:** - Ultrasound is not every visit. NHS (UK) says: "You'll be offered at least 10 antenatal appointments if this is your first baby, and 2 ultrasound scans." So step 4 is mostly the heartbeat and belly check; keep the ultrasound as an occasional scene.
**Watchman:** - UK vs US: in the UK the visit is usually with a midwife (NHS: "You'll usually see a midwife, and also a doctor if you need to."). US pages (ACOG, Mayo) talk about an ob-gyn or other provider. The game can keep "doctor" and "nurse".
**Watchman:** - No source I opened covers Thailand practice, so no US vs Thailand note.
**Watchman:** - Visits get closer together near the end (ACOG: "you may visit your ob-gyn more often"). "Come back in two weeks" is a sample line, not a medical schedule.

**Watchman:** ### What varies
**Watchman:** - Visitor rules vary by hospital: Kaweah says "Patients are allowed one (1) visitor in the triage room"; UPMC says "You can also have up to two support people, plus a doula". So "Can Sam stay with me?" fits everywhere.
**Watchman:** - Length of stay (UPMC): "Two days if you have a vaginal birth. Three to four days if you have a cesarean section."
**Watchman:** - Call first: UPMC says to call the obstetrician's line when labor starts. A game scene could open with Sam making that call.
